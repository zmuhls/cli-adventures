      Tips
      • Click Bit, the little sprite by the prompt, for a hint. Click again for a bigger one.
      • Press ↑ and ↓ to bring back earlier commands. Tab finishes names; on an empty prompt it moves focus on.
      • Purple names ending in / are directories. Names that start with a dot are hidden; ls -a shows them.
      • ~ is short for your home directory, /home/fsociety.
      • When the agent asks allow? [y/n], read exactly what it wants to run before you answer.
      • Ctrl + L clears the screen. reset restarts the chapter.
      • Expand the terminal with the button in its tab bar; Esc restores it.
